A Metabase driver for InterSystems IRIS that I built during my internship at InterSystems, written in Clojure. It connects Metabase, an open-source BI platform, to IRIS databases over JDBC, so teams can build dashboards and analytics on their IRIS data.

The driver is listed on InterSystems Open Exchange and in Metabase's official [community drivers documentation](https://github.com/metabase/metabase/pull/61431), and the client it was built for adopted it.
