Built in a team of three for a module during my exchange at TUM. Glasshouse profiles an exported ChatGPT or Claude history with Gemini to show what an AI provider could infer about you. Every finding must cite a quote that the backend checks against your own messages, and reports stay in your browser. I wrote the app code; my teammates drafted the prompts and taxonomy.
